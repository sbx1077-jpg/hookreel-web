Our Story

Where Southern Bold Flavors Meet Backyard Boil Traditions

With a passion for bringing the vibrant flavors of the South to guests across the country, Hook & Reel celebrates the time-honored tradition of seafood boils that originated in Louisiana backyard gatherings. Inspired by the rich culinary heritage of the Gulf Coast, our journey began with a desire to create a dining experience that combines the bold, zesty flavors of Southern cuisine with the fun, communal spirit of a classic boil.

At Hook & Reel, we're proud to serve seafood lovers from coast to coast! With locations spanning the nation, our commitment to bringing you the freshest seafood boils is stronger than ever. Whether you're on the sunny shores of California or the bustling streets of New York, you can savor the rich flavors and vibrant experience of our signature seafood boils.

Indulge in a feast of succulent shrimp, tender crab, and perfectly spiced sausage, all tossed together with our special blend of seasonings and served piping hot. Our seafood boils are designed to bring friends and family together, creating unforgettable moments and delicious memories no matter where you are.

Visit us at one of our locations today and experience the joy of a seafood boil in your own backyard — no matter where you call home!
